GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Study abroad FAQs.

A useful starting point for your planning. Requirements differ by institution and country, so confirm your individual situation with the official source.

Explore our resource corner ↗
What does Eduforn help students with?+

Eduforn’s website describes support with counselling, course and institution selection, applications, visa documentation and preparation, and pre-departure planning. The exact services available can depend on your destination and profile.

How do I choose the right country, course and university?+

Start with your academic background, career goals, budget, preferred learning environment and eligibility. Compare course content and institution entry requirements before deciding; a counsellor can help you build a shortlist.

When should I start planning my study abroad application?+

Start well ahead of your intended intake. Application, language testing, document preparation, funding and visa steps take different amounts of time. Check the dates published by your chosen institution and government.

Which documents are usually needed to apply to a university?+

Common documents include academic transcripts and certificates, identity documents, a CV or statement, references and evidence of English proficiency where required. Requirements vary by course and institution; use its official checklist.

Do I need IELTS, PTE or another English test?+

It depends on the institution, course, destination and your prior education. Some providers accept multiple tests or have other ways to meet English requirements. Confirm the accepted test and minimum scores directly with the institution.

Can I get a scholarship or education loan?+

Scholarships and funding are set by institutions, governments and lenders. Eligibility, award amounts and deadlines vary. Check official scholarship pages and lenders’ terms, and budget for costs that are not covered.

What is the Student Direct Stream (SDS) for Canada?+

Canada ended SDS on November 8, 2024. New study permit applications use the regular process. Some applicants may need a provincial or territorial attestation letter; check the latest IRCC study permit guidance.

Can international students work while studying?+

Work rights and limits depend on the country, visa conditions, course and individual eligibility. For Canada, eligible students may generally work up to 24 hours per week off campus during regular academic sessions, subject to official conditions. Check current IRCC eligibility and conditions before working.

Does admission guarantee a student visa?+

No. Admission and a visa decision are separate. Only the relevant government authority decides visa applications, and requirements and outcomes depend on your circumstances. No counsellor can guarantee approval.

How do I check current visa requirements for my destination?+

Use the government’s official immigration website for your destination and check requirements for your own situation. Visit the relevant Canada, US, UK, Australia, Ireland, New Zealand, Dubai, Singapore or Europe page for a starting point.

What happens in a counselling session?+

You can discuss your study goals, education history, budget, timeline and questions. Eduforn can then explain possible next steps and services relevant to your plans.
