Canada study permit after SDS: what changed?

The Student Direct Stream (SDS) ended on November 8, 2024. New applicants should use the regular study permit process and rely on IRCC’s current document checklist for their individual case.

Draft author: Eduforn Editorial TeamUpdated October 4, 2026 · reviewer to be assigned

Older articles, videos and checklists may still describe SDS-specific steps. Those pages can be outdated. Canada’s immigration department ended the SDS and Nigeria Student Express (NSE) on November 8, 2024. Applications received before the closure date continued to be processed under the applicable instructions; new applications go through the regular study permit process.

What should a new applicant check?

Start with the current IRCC study permit page. Check that the school is a designated learning institution and that the intended program is eligible for the study plan. Review the latest application steps, required forms, fees and evidence of financial support before preparing documents. A school admission letter does not itself guarantee a study permit.

Many applicants must include a provincial attestation letter (PAL) or territorial attestation letter (TAL), but IRCC lists exceptions and rules can change. The letter is generally provided through the province or territory and the designated institution. Quebec applicants may have separate Quebec Acceptance Certificate requirements. Use IRCC’s current PAL/TAL instructions to check the requirement that applies to you instead of relying on an old universal checklist.

Can international students work while studying?

Eligible international students may generally work off campus up to 24 hours per week during regular academic sessions, subject to IRCC’s conditions. Eligibility depends on factors such as the study permit, enrollment and program. Scheduled breaks have separate rules. Read the live IRCC guidance and your own permit conditions before accepting work; do not rely on old “20 hours” advice or assume every student qualifies.

A practical preparation checklist

  • Confirm the institution and program details using official school sources.
  • Use IRCC’s current document and financial-support lists for your situation.
  • Check whether a PAL/TAL or Quebec document applies and how to obtain it.
  • Keep copies of what you submit and review your permit conditions after a decision.
