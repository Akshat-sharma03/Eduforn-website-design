US F-1 student visa: steps after getting accepted

A practical overview of the U.S. F-1 student visa sequence, from school certification to interview preparation.

Eduforn Resource CornerOfficial sources checked October 2026

Short answer

After admission, an F-1 applicant generally needs a place at an SEVP-certified school, Form I-20, the required SEVIS fee payment, a DS-160 application and a visa interview. Follow the U.S. government instructions for your location and case.

What to check

Confirm that the school is authorized to enroll international students and request your Form I-20 after meeting the school’s requirements. Review the form carefully and ask the school to correct any errors.

Use the current Department of State instructions to complete the DS-160, pay required fees and book an interview where required. Prepare evidence that addresses the questions on the official student-visa guidance; document requirements can vary.

A visa does not guarantee admission at the border, and admission to a school does not guarantee visa issuance. The consular officer decides the application. Never rely on a third party to promise an outcome.

Common question

Does admission guarantee an F-1 visa?

No. The U.S. government makes the visa decision after considering the individual application.

Official sources

  • U.S. Department of State: Student Visa
  • Study in the States: School search

This guide is general planning information, not legal or immigration advice. Requirements can change and depend on your circumstances. Confirm current details with official authorities and your chosen institution.
