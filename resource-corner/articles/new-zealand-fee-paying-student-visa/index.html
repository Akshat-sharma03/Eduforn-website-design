New Zealand Fee Paying Student Visa: eligibility and evidence

A starting guide to New Zealand’s Fee Paying Student Visa, including offer-of-place and evidence checks.

Eduforn Resource CornerOfficial sources checked October 2026

Short answer

The Fee Paying Student Visa is for eligible students paying fees to study in New Zealand. Applicants generally need an offer of place from an approved provider and must meet current identity, funds, health and character requirements.

What to check

Confirm the course and provider and obtain an offer of place that meets Immigration New Zealand’s requirements. Check the visa page for age, duration, fees and application timing that apply to you.

Prepare evidence of tuition payment or ability to pay, living costs and onward travel as required. Health, character and insurance rules may also apply, depending on your stay and circumstances.

Apply using the official online process and respond promptly to requests. Work rights, if granted, are subject to visa conditions; check the eVisa and official guidance.

Common question

Can I work on this visa?

Some students may have work conditions. Check the specific visa conditions and current official rules.
