Singapore Student’s Pass: who needs one and how to apply

Find out when international students need a Singapore Student’s Pass and how to verify the application sequence.

Eduforn Resource CornerOfficial sources checked October 2026

Short answer

Foreign students accepted for full-time study at an eligible Singapore institution generally need a Student’s Pass unless an exemption applies. The institution and ICA’s current instructions guide the application sequence and timing.

What to check

Confirm that the course is full-time and the institution is eligible to sponsor or support the Student’s Pass application. Part-time, evening or weekend courses may be treated differently.

Follow the institution’s instructions to begin the application through the official system, submit accurate identity and supporting documents, and meet the stated deadlines. Processing steps and required documents can vary.

Do not assume a visit pass is equivalent to a Student’s Pass. Check the approval and issuance steps, any medical requirements, and the conditions attached to your pass before travelling or starting classes.

Common question

Can I study on a tourist visit?

Do not assume so. Check the pass required for your course and circumstances with ICA and your institution.

This guide is general planning information, not legal or immigration advice. Requirements can change and depend on your circumstances. Confirm current details with official authorities and your chosen institution.
