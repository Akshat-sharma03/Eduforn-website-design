AUSTRALIA · STUDENT VISA

Australia Subclass 500 student visa: what to prepare

Learn the core preparation points for an Australian Student visa (subclass 500) application and verify current requirements.

Eduforn Resource CornerOfficial sources checked October 2026

Short answer

A Student visa (subclass 500) application is tied to an eligible course and Confirmation of Enrolment. Applicants should check current document, health, character, English and Genuine Student requirements that apply to their individual case.

What to check

Confirm the course and provider, receive your Confirmation of Enrolment, and review the Department of Home Affairs document checklist for your passport country and circumstances.

Prepare evidence requested for your application, which may include identity, funds, English ability, health and character information. The Genuine Student questions ask you to explain your circumstances and study intentions truthfully.

Lodge through the official system and monitor messages for additional requests. Visa conditions and work rights depend on current law and the visa granted; read the official conditions rather than relying on general summaries.

Common question

Does every student submit identical evidence?

No. Check the document checklist and instructions generated for your own application.

Official sources

  • Australian Department of Home Affairs: Student visa (subclass 500)

This guide is general planning information, not legal or immigration advice. Requirements can change and depend on your circumstances. Confirm current details with official authorities and your chosen institution.
