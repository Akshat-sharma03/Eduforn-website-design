EUROPE · DESTINATION PLANNING

Study in Europe: compare countries, courses and funding

A practical framework for comparing European study destinations, course language, costs, funding and national visa rules.

Eduforn Resource CornerOfficial sources checked October 2026

Short answer

Europe is not one admissions or visa system. Compare countries and institutions individually, then confirm course, tuition, language, scholarship and immigration requirements through the provider and national authorities.

What to check

Start with subject fit and language of instruction. Check the course page for entry requirements, qualification recognition, teaching language, calendar and whether the institution is officially recognized in that country.

Build a realistic budget that includes tuition, housing, insurance, transport and application costs. Search official scholarship portals and institution funding pages; eligibility and deadlines vary.

After choosing a country, check its national immigration authority for student visa or residence requirements. The European Union’s study portal is a discovery resource, not a substitute for national rules.

Common question

Is there one student visa for all of Europe?

No. Immigration requirements are set by individual countries; verify with the destination country’s authority.

Official sources

  • European Commission: Study in Europe
  • European Commission: Scholarships and funding

This guide is general planning information, not legal or immigration advice. Requirements can change and depend on your circumstances. Confirm current details with official authorities and your chosen institution.
