Study abroad scholarships and education loans: research safely

A safe checklist for researching study abroad scholarships, education loans and funding claims.

Eduforn Resource CornerOfficial sources checked October 2026

Short answer

Start with official university, government and recognized lender sources. Compare eligibility, total cost, repayment terms and deadlines, and never pay an unverified agent a fee to “secure” a scholarship or guaranteed loan.

What to check

Search the destination government, institution and recognized scholarship-provider pages. Record eligibility, covered costs, renewal rules, application materials and deadlines. Scholarships can be competitive and may cover only part of the cost.

For loans, compare the annual percentage rate or equivalent, fees, currency, collateral or co-signer requirements, repayment start date, moratorium, exchange-rate exposure and early-payment terms. Ask the lender for a written schedule.

Verify the recipient domain and contact the institution using details from its official website. Treat guaranteed awards, urgency, advance payment demands and requests for passwords or one-time codes as warning signs.

Common question

Can an adviser guarantee a scholarship or loan?

No. Awards are made by scholarship providers and loans are subject to lender approval and terms.

This guide is general planning information, not legal or immigration advice. Requirements can change and depend on your circumstances. Confirm current details with official authorities and your chosen institution.
