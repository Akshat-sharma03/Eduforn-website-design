Ireland long-stay study visa: application preparation

Understand the official steps and documents for planning an Irish long-stay study visa application.

Eduforn Resource CornerOfficial sources checked October 2026

Short answer

Students planning a course longer than 90 days may need a long-stay study visa depending on nationality. Ireland’s immigration service lists the online application and supporting evidence requirements; check the latest rules for your case.

What to check

Check whether your nationality requires a visa and whether your chosen course/provider meets current immigration requirements. Begin with the official long-stay study visa instructions and the course list where applicable.

Complete the online application, follow the summary sheet instructions, and prepare the required evidence. This can include acceptance, fees, finances, academic history, English ability, insurance and explanations of any gaps, as specified by the current guidance.

A visa is not a residence permission by itself for every step of a long stay. Follow instructions after arrival and check registration requirements and deadlines with Irish Immigration.

Common question

Do all students need a visa?

Visa need depends on nationality and length/purpose of stay. Use Irish Immigration’s current guidance.

Official sources

  • Irish Immigration: Apply for a long-term study visa
  • Irish Immigration: Study visa options

This guide is general planning information, not legal or immigration advice. Requirements can change and depend on your circumstances. Confirm current details with official authorities and your chosen institution.
