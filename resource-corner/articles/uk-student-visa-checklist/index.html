UNITED KINGDOM · STUDENT VISA

UK Student visa checklist: documents and application basics

Review the main UK Student visa preparation steps and check the current official document rules before applying.

Eduforn Resource CornerOfficial sources checked October 2026

Short answer

Most applicants need a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies from a licensed student sponsor, a valid passport and evidence required for their situation. Additional financial, language, consent or health documents may apply. Check GOV.UK for your exact case.

What to check

Start with the CAS issued by your education provider and make sure the course and personal details are correct. Apply within the permitted window and use the current GOV.UK application process.

Check whether you must show funds, English-language evidence, an ATAS certificate, parental consent or other documents. The evidence rules depend on circumstances and can change; use the official checklist, not an old online template.

Prepare clear copies and translations where required. Keep application references and follow the official instructions for biometrics and any requested follow-up.

Common question

Can I use a generic checklist?

Use it only as a planning aid. GOV.UK’s requirements for your circumstances are authoritative.
