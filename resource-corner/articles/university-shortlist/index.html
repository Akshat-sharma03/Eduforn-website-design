How to shortlist universities abroad: a step-by-step checklist

A strong shortlist starts with course fit and verified entry requirements—not a ranking alone. Use this process to compare options against your goals, academic profile and budget.

Draft author: Eduforn Editorial TeamUpdated October 4, 2026 · reviewer to be assigned

1. Define what you want to study

Write down the subject area, qualification level and career direction you have in mind. Compare course modules, practical learning, placement structure and the qualification awarded. Similar course titles can cover different material, so read the current program page and course handbook carefully.

2. Check entry requirements against your profile

For each option, open the institution’s own admissions page. Check academic prerequisites, previous study, required subjects, minimum grades, English-language evidence, portfolio or work experience requirements, and any country-specific qualifications. Note the application route and deadlines for the exact intake. If an entry requirement is unclear, ask the admissions office; a general university page may not describe the rules for your program.

3. Compare the full cost

Look beyond tuition. Estimate living expenses, deposits, health coverage, transport, books, application and visa costs, and currency changes. Search official institution pages for international tuition and scholarships. Check funding criteria and deadlines directly with the institution or lender. Treat a scholarship, loan or part-time job as uncertain until formally confirmed.

4. Consider the place and student support

Compare campus location, commute, housing availability, climate, accessibility, academic support and student services. For international study, also check the school’s official international student support and the rules that apply in that country.

5. Build and verify a balanced shortlist

Keep a comparison sheet with the official course link, entry criteria, total estimated cost, application date, required documents, scholarship information and the date you last checked each item. Include options with different entry profiles and costs if they genuinely suit your goals. Revisit the list before applying because programs, fees and dates may change.
