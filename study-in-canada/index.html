GOOD QUESTIONS

Start with the basics.

For personal eligibility and the latest rules, always check official IRCC information.

Where can I check study permit requirements?+

Requirements depend on your situation and may change. Review current IRCC study permit guidance before applying.

Can international students work while studying?+

Eligibility and conditions vary. Review current IRCC off-campus work guidance or speak with a qualified advisor about your situation.

How do I choose a Canadian school?+

Compare program fit, admissions requirements, location, tuition and other costs. Confirm the institution and program details through official sources before applying.

Does Eduforn guarantee a study permit?+

No. Eduforn can provide counselling and application support, but only Canadian authorities decide study permit applications.
