Privacy notice for this preview.

This is a working draft for review by Eduforn. It is not a final privacy policy or legal advice.

Local testing form

The form in this preview is client-side only. Its submit handler prevents submission and displays a local message; it is not connected to an enquiry service. Do not enter sensitive personal, financial or immigration information.

Contacting Eduforn

If you choose to call or email Eduforn, your communication is handled through those channels and their providers. Confirm Eduforn’s actual data handling, retention, access and rights process before publishing a final notice.

Hosting and site analytics

Before production, Eduforn should confirm the hosting provider’s logs, any analytics or cookies, processors, retention periods and contact for privacy requests. This preview does not claim that these matters have been legally reviewed.

Company review checklist

  • Confirm controller identity and privacy contact.
  • Describe the actual enquiry workflow and lawful basis.
  • Document retention, security, service providers and cross-border transfers.
  • Explain applicable user rights and complaint channels.

Do not treat this draft as a published compliance statement.
